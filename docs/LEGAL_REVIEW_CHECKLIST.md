# Legal review checklist

**All legal documents in this repository are drafts pending review by qualified counsel.** Completion of a checklist row is not a licence or regulatory approval.

## Corporate and product structure

- [ ] Identify operating entity, owners, directors and beneficial owners.
- [ ] Obtain written classification of P2P challenges and platform role.
- [ ] Confirm enforceability of participant-to-participant wagering terms.
- [ ] Decide whether a licensed-operator partnership is preferable to direct operation.
- [ ] Document every target jurisdiction and disable all unapproved territories.

## Ghana gaming

- [ ] Confirm licence category and application route with the Gaming Commission.
- [ ] Confirm capital, technical, premises, fit-and-proper, reporting and audit conditions.
- [ ] Obtain approval for supported sports, markets and any live functionality.
- [ ] Confirm minimum age, identity, location and exclusion requirements.
- [ ] Review advertising, sponsorship, affiliates and influencer language.
- [ ] Obtain tax advice for fees, winnings, withholding, VAT and reporting.

## Payments and customer funds

- [ ] Obtain counsel and provider confirmation for every payment method.
- [ ] Document custody/safeguarding and no-commingling arrangement.
- [ ] Confirm chargeback, refund, failed-payment and withdrawal obligations.
- [ ] Ensure ledger, provider report and bank/safeguarding reconciliation is tested.
- [ ] Confirm no demo credit can be purchased, transferred, redeemed or exchanged.

## AML, integrity and complaints

- [ ] Appoint accountable compliance/MLRO roles and escalation coverage.
- [ ] Approve CDD/EDD, sanctions, transaction monitoring and suspicious activity procedures.
- [ ] Define collusion, manipulation, multi-account and insider-abuse controls.
- [ ] Define complaints, disputes, result challenges and regulator access procedures.
- [ ] Define record retention and lawful disclosure process.

## Privacy and cyber

- [ ] Complete data map, DPIA, privacy notice and lawful-basis review.
- [ ] Approve identity/vendor processor agreements and cross-border transfer controls.
- [ ] Define retention, deletion, access/correction and breach response.
- [ ] Complete threat model, penetration test, independent code/security review and DR test.

## Evidence package

For each checked item attach the document ID, approval authority, effective date, expiry/review date, scope, related test case and deployment version. Legal drafts must remain labelled `DRAFT — PENDING QUALIFIED COUNSEL REVIEW` until formally replaced.
