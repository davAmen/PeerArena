# Five-year financial model (illustrative)

**This is a planning model, not a forecast, investment advice or promise of profitability.** All amounts below are illustrative **GHS thousands** for a future authorized operation. The current demo has zero real revenue, zero customer funds and zero real-money exposure.

## Assumptions

- Revenue is modeled as an illustrative blended platform take rate on settled gross challenge volume, subject to legal and provider approval.
- The take rate is not implemented in the demo and must not be treated as an approved fee.
- Operating costs include engineering, hosting, data, identity/compliance, support, legal, security, marketing and payment/chargeback overhead.
- Customer funds and gross challenge volume are not company revenue; the ledger must keep them separate.
- Scenarios are directional and should be replaced with cohort, retention, market-size, provider-quote and regulatory-cost evidence.

The moderate illustration remains investment-heavy because licensing, compliance, data, support and trust controls are expensive relative to early take-rate revenue. It should not be used to claim that consumer operation is commercially viable. A B2B licensing or regulated-operator partnership could have a different cost and revenue profile.

## Sensitivities to validate

- Customer acquisition cost and organic share.
- Monthly active users, challenge frequency and average stake.
- Match rate, cancellation/void rate and repeat behavior.
- Provider and payment pricing, chargebacks and refunds.
- Licence, capital, tax, insurance and audit cost.
- Support contacts per active account and responsible-play interventions.
- Fraud loss, collusion loss and payout reserve.
- Country-specific legal restrictions and market availability.

## Model governance

Store assumptions with version, source, owner and review date. Finance must reconcile volume to the double-entry ledger; customer stakes are liabilities/flow-through amounts, not revenue. Any proposed fee change requires legal/compliance approval and a customer-facing disclosure update.
