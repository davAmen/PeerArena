# Regulatory requirements matrix

This matrix is a work product for counsel and regulated partners. “Open” means the engineering team must not treat the item as satisfied by the demo.

| Area | Ghana question / control | Evidence required before launch | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gaming classification | Does P2P sports prediction fall within a licensed gaming activity, and which category? | Written legal opinion plus Gaming Commission correspondence | Legal / compliance | Open |
| Licence | What entity, capital, premises, technical and reporting requirements apply? | Licence application pack, approval and conditions | Board / legal | Open |
| Eligible users | Minimum age, identity, resident status and prohibited jurisdictions | KYC/age vendor evidence, geo policy and test results | Compliance | Open |
| Market scope | Which sports, markets, live features and non-sport categories are approved? | Market catalogue approval and integrity review | Compliance / trading | Open |
| Data rights | Can each provider's data be used for display and settlement? | Executed commercial data contract and SLA | Data / legal | Open |
| Result verification | What is official, what is the review window, and when can a result be reversed? | Settlement rulebook and dispute procedure | Trading / compliance | Open |
| Payments | Is the PSP/EMI authorized for the proposed gaming flow? | Provider approval, contracts, flow-of-funds diagram | Finance / legal | Open |
| Safeguarding | Where are customer funds held and how are they separated? | Account structure, reconciliation controls and audit report | Finance | Open |
| AML/CFT | CDD, EDD, sanctions, transaction monitoring and reporting obligations | Policies, vendor validation, sample cases and records | MLRO / compliance | Open |
| Responsible gambling | Limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, support and marketing restrictions | Approved policy, UX evidence, support SOP and test cases | Responsible-play lead | Open |
| Privacy | Lawful basis, notices, DPIA, retention, processor and rights process | Counsel-approved privacy package and DPIA | DPO / legal | Open |
| Cybersecurity | Incident response, access control, logging, resilience and testing | Security assessment, pen test, DR test and remediation sign-off | CISO / CTO | Open |
| Consumer protection | Terms, fees, complaints, refunds, voids and dispute transparency | Approved terms, contact process and complaint register | Legal / support | Open |
| Tax | Gaming, withholding, VAT or other applicable obligations | Tax opinion and reporting calendar | Finance / tax | Open |
| Advertising | No misleading, guaranteed-income or vulnerable-user targeting claims | Approved campaign checklist and monitoring | Marketing / compliance | Open |
| International expansion | Is each new country separately approved? | Country-by-country legal and technical gate | Board / legal | Not enabled |

## Evidence protocol

Each completed row needs a signed owner, document identifier, effective date, expiry/review date, affected product version and test evidence. A screenshot or verbal approval is not sufficient for a production gate. Changes to licence scope, providers, markets, fees, settlement rules or customer-funds flow reopen the relevant review.
