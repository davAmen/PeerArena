# DRAFT — PRIVACY POLICY

**Pending review by a qualified Ghana privacy counsel / data protection officer. This draft is not a notice for production collection.**

## 1. Scope

This draft describes the categories and purposes anticipated for a future authorized PeerArena service. The current demo uses synthetic data in memory and does not collect identity documents, payment credentials or real customer funds.

## 2. Data categories

- Account data: username, email/phone contact, country, language and preferences.
- Verification data: age/identity status and vendor reference; sensitive evidence should remain with an approved processor where possible.
- Activity data: challenges, predictions, results, limits, self-exclusion, support and complaint records.
- Transaction data: ledger references, provider references, amounts, fees, refunds, chargebacks and reconciliation state.
- Security data: session, device, IP, risk signals, access and audit events.
- Technical data: browser, app, error and performance information.

## 3. Purposes

Account access, eligibility, responsible-play controls, challenge operation, settlement, customer support, fraud/AML and integrity controls, security, legal obligations, complaints, audit, service improvement and regulatory reporting. Marketing requires a separate lawful basis and must respect exclusion and communication preferences.

## 4. Legal basis and notices

The operator must document the applicable lawful basis for each purpose, provide a clear collection notice, identify the controller and processors, explain international transfers, and obtain any required consent. This draft does not select a final legal basis.

## 5. Sharing

Data may be shared with approved identity, payment, sports-data, hosting, security, support, professional-adviser and regulatory recipients only as authorized and necessary. Public profiles use pseudonyms; private identity evidence is not displayed in the marketplace.

## 6. Retention

Retention must be purpose- and law-based. Financial, AML, exclusion, complaint and audit records may require longer retention than ordinary product analytics. A schedule must specify duration, archive, deletion/erasure exception and owner.

## 7. Rights and requests

The production service must provide a verified route for access, correction, objection, restriction, deletion where applicable, portability and complaint/escalation rights. Requests must not delete records that the operator is legally required to preserve.

## 8. Security and breach response

Use access control, encryption, minimization, processor governance, monitoring, backups and tested incident response. Suspected breaches must be contained, documented and notified to the appropriate authority and affected people where required.

## 9. Children and vulnerable people

The service is not intended for under-age participation. Age/identity/geographic controls and responsible-play safeguards must be active before any regulated access. Marketing must not target vulnerable users.

## 10. Changes and contact

The final notice will name the operator, DPO/contact, supervisory authority route, effective date, version history and jurisdiction-specific terms. No production collection should begin using this draft alone.
