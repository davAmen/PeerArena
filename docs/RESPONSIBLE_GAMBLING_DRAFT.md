# DRAFT — RESPONSIBLE GAMBLING POLICY

**Pending review by qualified counsel, the relevant regulator and an appropriately qualified responsible-gambling specialist. The current demo uses no real money.**

## Our position

PeerArena must not present betting as income, investment, recovery of losses or a guaranteed outcome. The current demo labels every balance as fictional and provides no deposit, withdrawal or redemption path.

## Required controls for an authorized service

- Legal-age and identity verification before participation.
- Jurisdiction eligibility and exclusion enforcement before each material action.
- Deposit, stake, loss, session and time limits with clear customer control.
- Temporary time-out and permanent self-exclusion with immediate action blocking.
- Self-exclusion propagation to linked accounts and marketing suppression.
- Session reminders, spending statements and clear exposure/potential-loss disclosures.
- No credit, unauthorized overdraft or pressure to increase stakes.
- Cooling-off before selected limit increases where required by policy or law.
- Support referrals and independent gambling-help resources appropriate to the user's location.
- Staff training, escalation, complaints and incident records.
- Marketing review that excludes vulnerable audiences and avoids misleading urgency.

## User flow

Safety controls are available from the banner, navigation, account and help surfaces. A user can enable self-exclusion without contacting support. Once active, create and accept commands fail closed; existing challenges follow the approved handling rule and the user can still access statements, support and complaints.

## Detection and intervention

Monitor patterns such as rapid stake escalation, chasing losses, long sessions, repeated failed limits, self-exclusion evasion, unusual hours, coordinated accounts and high-risk payment behavior. Automated signals may prompt an education or support intervention, but must not be used to pressure additional play. High-risk cases go to trained staff under a documented process.

## Product language rules

Do not use “guaranteed profit,” “easy money,” “risk-free,” “win back losses,” “act now” pressure or status rewards that encourage excessive activity. Show both sides of a challenge, stake at risk, pool, fees, settlement source, void rules and the fact that a participant may lose the stake.

## Review and evidence

The responsible-gambling owner reviews controls, exclusion tests, customer contacts, complaints, marketing samples and intervention outcomes on a scheduled basis. Changes require approval, versioning and audit evidence.
